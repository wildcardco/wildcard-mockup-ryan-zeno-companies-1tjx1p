About Zeno

A Chesterton shop since 2013

Zeno Companies is a print shop, design studio and website designer working out of 102 Brown Ave in Chesterton.

No job is too small. One customer came in for a single delivery sign for the house; others need graphics on a full fleet of trucks. Either way you talk to the people doing the work, see options side by side, and get a finished piece you can pick up fast.

Because design, printing, signs, vehicle graphics and websites all happen in one place, your brand stays consistent from the front door to the side of the van.
